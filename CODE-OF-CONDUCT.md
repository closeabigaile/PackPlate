# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to be a productive, yet friendly group. We want to work well together,
all contribute, and help other group members when we can and when it is
needed. We are kind, open, and welcoming to every member.

## Our standards

**Everyone:**

- Does their part and contributes to the project.
- Helps teammates when they can and when it is needed.
- Attends group meetings unless they genuinely cannot.
- Critiques the code, not the person, and settles disagreements by discussion
  or a team vote.

**Missed meetings:**

- If you cannot attend a meeting, you must make an effort to find out what was
  discussed.
- If you attend a meeting that someone missed, you must tell them the important
  details.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, bullying, being rude to others, or any other
behavior that makes a teammate feel unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies in group meetings, in our team chat, and on GitHub (issues,
pull requests, and code reviews).

## Reporting and enforcement

1. **Small or teammate-to-teammate issues** are first resolved privately,
   through direct messages between the people involved.
2. **If an issue escalates, continues, or is a group problem**, it moves to an
   email thread with the professor. Every member except the member in question
   is on the thread, and the issue is discussed there.

If a member does not follow this code, the group first talks with that member.
If the behavior does not change, the group takes it up with the teaching
staff, as above.

**All reports are kept confidential.**
